# Ammamma Foods - Color Palette

## New Warm Golden Palette

This website uses a beautiful, warm earth-tone color palette inspired by Indian spices and natural ingredients. The palette creates a pleasant, inviting, and desirable aesthetic that encourages engagement and purchasing.

## Color Breakdown

### Primary Colors

**Primary (Warm Brown-Gold)**
- Light: HSL(35, 65%, 42%) - `#9B7C3B`
- Used for: CTAs, buttons, interactive elements, headers
- Effect: Warm, sophisticated, trustworthy

**Secondary (Golden Orange)**
- Light: HSL(45, 85%, 55%) - `#D4A049`
- Used for: Accents, highlights, badges
- Effect: Energetic, inviting, appetizing

**Accent (Bright Yellow)**
- Light: HSL(45, 100%, 60%) - `#FFD700`
- Used for: Special offers, highlights, hover states
- Effect: Attention-grabbing, premium feel

### Neutral Colors

**Background**
- Light: HSL(40, 25%, 96%) - `#F5F1EB`
- Dark: HSL(35, 30%, 12%) - `#3D2D1F`
- Effect: Warm, natural, inviting

**Foreground/Text**
- Light: HSL(35, 35%, 25%) - `#5C4A34`
- Dark: HSL(40, 20%, 92%) - `#EBE7E0`
- Effect: Easy to read, warm undertones

### Additional Colors

**Muted (Soft Beige)**
- HSL(40, 20%, 92%) - `#EBE7E0`
- Used for: Disabled states, subtle backgrounds

**Border (Light Brown)**
- HSL(40, 20%, 88%) - `#DFD8CF`
- Used for: Dividers, card borders, subtle separation

## Why This Palette?

1. **Warm & Inviting** - The golden and brown tones create comfort and trust
2. **Spice Inspired** - Colors evoke natural spices and traditional Indian ingredients
3. **Premium Feel** - The palette suggests quality and authenticity
4. **High Contrast** - Excellent readability and accessibility
5. **Versatile** - Works well in light and dark modes

## Usage Guidelines

- Use **Primary** for main CTAs and interactive elements
- Use **Secondary** for supporting actions and accents
- Use **Accent** sparingly for sales/special offers (less is more)
- Use **Background** for page backgrounds and base colors
- Use **Foreground** for all text and content
- Use **Muted** for secondary text and disabled states

## Component Examples

### Buttons
- Primary Buttons: Primary background with white text
- Secondary Buttons: Secondary background with foreground text
- Ghost Buttons: Transparent with primary border

### Cards
- White background (HSL(0, 0%, 100%))
- Warm foreground text
- Subtle border in light brown
- Creates clean, premium appearance

### Hover States
- Buttons: Slightly darker shade of original color
- Cards: Subtle shadow increase
- Accent color for active states

## Results

With this palette applied across the website:
- Homepage feels warm and welcoming
- Products appear more desirable
- CTAs stand out naturally (not jarring)
- User experience is pleasant and cohesive
- Brand identity is consistent throughout
